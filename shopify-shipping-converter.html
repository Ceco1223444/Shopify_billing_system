    How it works · adding product photos in Excel

    Import

    1. In Shopify: Orders → Export, choose a CSV option. Drop one or more files above.
    2. Columns are matched by their names, so their order in the file does not matter.
    3. Every product row is kept. Orders are grouped by customer: the same email (spaces and capital letters ignored), or the same phone number and the same name. Separate orders are never merged.
    4. Yellow, in the preview and in the workbook: customers with more than one order, and orders with more than one product (several product rows, or a quantity above 1).
    5. Order details that Shopify writes only on an order's first row are not copied to its other product rows — only identifiers (order number, email, recipient) are repeated, so amounts such as Total appear once per order.
    6. The workbook has Product Photo plus 18 columns, every cell outlined. Cancelled orders, customer notes, deliveries abroad, company deliveries and orders where the amount still to pay differs from Total are still checked and listed under warnings.
    7. Nothing is deleted automatically. Repeated imports and suspected duplicates are flagged; you can exclude a repeated copy yourself.

    Adding photos in Excel

    1. The first column, Product Photo, is empty on purpose. Each of its cells is a square (about 80 × 80 pixels).
    2. Crop the photo to a square first (any photo app, or in Excel: Picture Format → Crop → Aspect Ratio → 1:1).
    3. Microsoft 365: select the photo cell, then Insert → Pictures → Place in Cell. The picture fits inside the cell and moves with sorting and filtering.
    4. Older Excel: Insert → Pictures, set height and width to 2.12 cm (0.83 in), drag it onto the cell, then Format Picture → Properties → Move and size with cells.
